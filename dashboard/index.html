AegisIQ Coaching Dashboard

Four sites, 6 Jan to 29 Jun 2025. Built from the star-schema warehouse (aegisiq.db).

Synthetic data. AegisIQ is a fictional course case. Every number here comes from data generated by etl/generate_sample_data.py, not from a real company.

1. Does more coaching improve MMR faster?

Average MMR gained per month, by how many coaching sessions a player gets per month.

2. Revenue per coaching hour, by site

Net revenue divided by hours actually coached (booked) and hours coaches were scheduled.

3. Coach utilization by week

Hours booked as a % of hours scheduled. Above 100% = overbooked; low = idle coaches.

4. Revenue and retention by plan

Net revenue per plan, with how long its players stay.
